A Better Theory of Inquiry

Most appraisals still pick a few comparables and rely on opaque adjustments. We model the market first, bridge mass-data depth with single-property rigor, and keep human principals in command of every judgment call.

PRINCIPLE

A few comps is a candle. We bring the whole market.

Appraisal is not the isolated measurement of a building. It is the disciplined interpretation of a market, with the subject property posed as a query to that market.

  • Pre-model market distributions before selecting comparables, narrowing judgment to what the evidence supports.
  • Corroborate facts across independent documentary records so every input is reproducible on cross-examination.
  • Deliver concise, table-forward reports where every adjustment is transparent and earned by convergence.

04 / PUBLIC SECTOR & MASS VALUATION

Better on the Front End.
Stronger on the Back End.

Taxing authorities face a structural divide: they must value thousands of commercial properties at scale each year, then defend individual assessments one by one in litigation.

GohnWood bridges that divide. We bring single-property commercial rigor upstream into front-end group valuations, and we bring mass-appraisal data depth into individual litigation defense.

INDEPENDENCE

We are not advocates. GohnWood serves both taxing authorities and property owners under governing law, with strict conflict screening across every engagement.

A

Front-End Commercial Cohort Valuation

Full-county and district-wide valuation models for commercial cohorts including hotels, multifamily, retail, and industrial assets under USPAP Standards 5 and 6. Designed to align market value with equal-and-uniform equity before notices issue.

B

Granular Benchmark Studies

Coarse third-party surveys assign three generic cap rates to dozens of distinct properties. We build empirical, sensitivity-tested cap-rate and operating-expense benchmarks directly from verified market datasets.

C

Assessment Office AI & Workflow Advisory

We help public assessment offices deploy privacy-locked, policy-compliant AI workflows to ingest owner operating statements and rent rolls, clean property records, retain institutional memory across staff turnover, and close recurring appeal gaps.
